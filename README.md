Solara Bay — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. A sun-soaked bay city to explore on foot and by car: 8 story missions with staged objectives, traffic and pedestrians, a wanted level with police who chase, ram and roadblock, a helicopter air unit, crew abilities, a night market, a beachfront, a full map/stats screen and a Dockside Customs workshop. Three.js, runs offline from static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

WASD drive & walk · E enter/exit car · SPACE brake · M mission · TAB map & stats · Z/X/C/V crew abilities · R radio · 1–6 weapons · mouse to shoot on foot. Progress autosaves in the browser.
